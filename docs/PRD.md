# Product Requirement Document

## Product
Alifito Rabbani Cahyono — Personal Branding Platform

## Objective
Establish a strong professional digital identity demonstrating technical capabilities, professional experience, engineering projects, leadership experience, achievements, and professional development for a Software Engineer & AI/ML Enthusiast.

## Target Audiences
- Software engineering recruiters
- Technical hiring managers
- Technology companies
- International employers
- Internship opportunities
- Scholarship committees
- Academic collaborators
- Professional networking
- Technical communities
- Potential clients and collaborators

## Core Dimensions
1. **Technical Excellence** — backend architecture, RESTful APIs, full-stack, databases, AI/ML, algorithms
2. **Leadership and Execution** — team leadership, stakeholder communication, project coordination, event management
3. **Professional Growth** — continuous learning, technical curiosity, adaptability

## Pages
- `/` Home — hero, introduction, featured projects, expertise, experience, leadership, timeline, achievements, insights, CTA
- `/about` — biography, academic journey, technical interests, engineering philosophy
- `/experience` — interactive career timeline with category filtering
- `/projects` — project listing with search, category and technology filters
- `/projects/[slug]` — full technical case study (20-section template)
- `/skills` — categorized skill display (no fake percentages)
- `/leadership` — organizational and event leadership showcase
- `/insights` — technical articles with categories, tags, reading time
- `/contact` — contact form with validation, rate limiting, anti-spam
- `/resume` — resume preview and PDF download

## Design
- Dark theme: Background #10141D, Primary #4386ED
- Light theme: Background #F4F3EF, Primary #456B9D
- Typography: Manrope (body), Space Grotesk (headings), JetBrains Mono (code)
- Concept: Modern Enterprise × Editorial Minimalism × Interactive Engineering

## Constraints
- No fabricated content, statistics, or social links
- No fake proficiency percentages
- No placeholder Lorem Ipsum
- WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility
- Core Web Vitals: LCP ≤ 2.5s, INP ≤ 200ms, CLS ≤ 0.1
- TypeScript strict mode
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion

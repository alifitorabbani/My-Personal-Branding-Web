# Content Guide

## Source of Truth
The CV (6-page PDF) is the primary source of truth. All content reflects verified information only.

## Content Rules
- No invented job positions, companies, periods, or responsibilities
- No fabricated statistics or performance metrics
- No fake certifications or awards
- No placeholder Lorem Ipsum in final interface
- Team achievements distinguished from individual contributions
- Confidential company data never exposed

## Sections
- **Experience** — professional, academic, organizational, committee
- **Projects** — 7 featured projects with full case study templates
- **Achievements** — 7 verified medals and recognitions
- **Skills** — 5 groups (backend, frontend, AI/ML, database, tools, professional)
- **Leadership** — organizational and event experience
- **Insights** — technical articles (drafts marked as pending content)

## Placeholders
Unavailable content (screenshots, demo URLs, architecture diagrams) is marked as "Pending content" and clearly labeled.
